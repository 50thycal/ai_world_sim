3. Observe

One decision per resident per simulated hour: 144 requests per day. Play runs the selected batch in series. Each fresh village continues until its final day, then the next begins. Pause stops the batch; restart always requires Play. A failed run stops the queue for review. Quiet idle/rest periods skip ahead. Existing runs retain their original rules and duration. Temporary timeouts retry up to twice after 2 and 5 seconds, using at most 12 extra attempts across the run. Other errors or exhausted recovery pause time. Play enables recovery on existing v4 runs. No API-key or automatic paid fallback. Subscription credits/settings still govern provider billing.
